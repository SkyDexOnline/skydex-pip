# SkyDex PIP

PIP is the gull from [Skydex](https://skydex.online), the free flight game over the real Earth, and this is his desktop app for Windows and Mac. He walks along the bottom of your screen, naps when you leave him alone, answers your clicks and flies off into the game when you take off.

**Download:** [skydex.online/pet](https://skydex.online/pet), or the files under [Releases](../../releases/latest) here.

This repository only holds the release files. The app's source is not public.

## What he does

- **Lives on his own.** He strolls, sits, plays and falls asleep. When he gets bored he hops to your other monitor or chases the mouse until you click him.
- **Answers you.** One click gets a hop and a heart, two a full turn, three a flip with confetti. Stroke him with the cursor and he melts. You can pick him up and put him anywhere.
- **Flies with you.** Link him to your Skydex account and he wears your PIP's colours and gear. Take off in the game and he joins you as your wingman; pause or land and he comes back. Or keep him on the desktop during flights: your catches pop over his head and **H** in the game sends him a heart.
- **Brings the news.** New rows in your Skydex bell show up as a bubble in your language (twelve of them).
- **Knows when to be quiet.** Active, Calm, Focus and Sleep modes. Over a fullscreen app he switches to Focus by himself.

## Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| Mac | macOS 12 Monterey or newer. Signed and notarized by Apple. |
| Windows | Windows 10 (21H1) or 11, 64-bit, DirectX 11. Not in the Microsoft Store yet, so SmartScreen may warn on the first start: **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |

On a Mac at rest he takes about 0.6% of the processor and 140 MB of memory.

## Privacy

He doesn't read your screen. To notice a fullscreen app he looks only at where windows are and how big they are, never at their titles or contents. Linking a computer creates a key that lets him read your PIP's look and your Skydex bell, nothing else; you can revoke it on [skydex.online/my/pet](https://skydex.online/my/pet). Anonymous usage stats (no account, no names) can be turned off in his menu.
